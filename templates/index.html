How Trinetra Works

From optical feeds on active city buses to real-time municipal dashboard analytics.

STAGE 01
Bus Camera Feed
High-definition optical frames continuously captured from standard cameras mounted on public transport fleets.
STAGE 02
Edge Computer Processing
In-vehicle computer vision inference models process video locally to pinpoint anomalies with <150ms latency.
STAGE 03
Telemetry Transmission
Lightweight event metadata and compressed video clips are transmitted to cloud command via cellular 5G networks.
STAGE 04
Spatial Analytics
Trinetra aggregates detections into live condition heatmaps, prioritizing public work repair orders dynamically.
